  - Boats: small boat in shallow water (ashore on foot within ten minutes, or head past 50 m depth), deep water,
    harbour or channel. Said plainly: this is for a local tsunami.
  - After: only go home when officials say so; stay away from the water at least 24 hours; property damage.

- App 14: Power cut: keep or throw out food (NZ). From MPI (emergencies page and the flood fact sheet's power

  Health Service, a Queenstown Lakes poster and Food Standards Australia New Zealand. One question at a time:
  - First screen: keep the fridge and freezer shut, eat from the fridge first, then the freezer, then the pantry.
  - Power just went off: one tap notes the time, then the app tells you when 2, 4, 24 and 48 hours have passed

  - While it's off: fridge and freezer times side by side (a small table by how full your freezer is), and a
    "come back later" screen, so you never lose your place.
  - Power back on: for each frozen food, "still has ice crystals?" Yes: refreeze. No: what kind of food, then
    what each source says. Then the fridge by hours without power, tins, and clean hands.
- Reviewer found 1 missed instruction ("check the label") and 14 findings, incl. a dead end if you finished
  while the power was still off. All fixed in one round.
