- App 6: Adult choking (ANZCOR Guideline 4 - Airway, section 4, approved April 2021). Start screen: signs of
  choking, then "Can they cough well?". Coughing well: reassure and keep them coughing; call an ambulance if it
  doesn't clear. Cough not working: send for an ambulance, up to 5 back blows, then up to 5 chest thrusts, back
  and forth until it clears (check after each one; abdominal thrusts are not recommended). Unresponsive: avoid
  blind finger sweeps, consider taking out anything you can see, and follow the Basic Life Support Flowchart if
  not breathing normally. Every loop has "The ambulance has arrived" as a way out. 8 short screens.
- Reviewer found 1 changed instruction (the app said "start CPR" where the guideline says "follow the Basic Life
  Support Flowchart") and 3 gaps that were only in hidden notes. All fixed in one round, plus a shorter first
  screen and one less tap before back blows.
