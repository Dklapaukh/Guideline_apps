Scheduled build run, 21 Nov 2026 (second run of the day, about 3 am).

Your feedback: nothing new on the design review page or on any app.

Built:
- App 19: Opioid overdose (NZ). Main source Healthify "Naloxone for opioid overdose" (Health NZ pharmacist
  reviewed, March 2026), with the NZ naloxone kit pamphlet (April 2026), Healthify "Opioid overdose" and The
  Level. Australian lines (NSW Health, health.gov.au) only where NZ says nothing, labelled on screen.
  - First screen: the signs, "don't assume they are asleep", call 111 and say you have naloxone. Then: kit, no
    kit, or not sure (not sure says use it anyway; it won't cause harm).
  - Kit route: safe place and recovery position, get the naloxone ready, inject, then a 3-minute wait timer and
    "breathing well, or waking?". Every dose is a Record tap, so the Log keeps the count and the clock time of
    each dose for paramedics. A one-off bar about 20 minutes after the first dose: naloxone may wear off.
  - Rescue breathing "2 breaths every 5 seconds" as quoted; still not breathing leads to another dose.
  - Woke up: stay with them, keep the ambulance coming, watch for it wearing off. Handover screen.
- Reviewer found 3 missed instructions (see the kit leaflet, stay with them, they may need medical help after
  naloxone) and 15 findings, the main ones: the wait timer was 2 minutes (now 3, the only time all three NZ
  sources allow); the 20-minute bar started before any dose; no "still not breathing" answer. All fixed.
- Scores: to fix 0, to check 0, all apps. 118 of 118 quotes exact (web copy). Tests: 38 of 38 pass.
- Seen working: phone-size screenshots of the start and get-ready screens (readable, nothing cut off).

Also: this run built app 18 too, but another run published it first, so this copy was dropped.

Your feedback round (same day): you asked for app additions clearly marked as the app's own (flow and tools,
no new advice), phone integration such as GPS, and a better design (less text per screen, more urgent, less
source clutter). Done:
- New "Tip:" line: the app's own flow or tool help, shown with an "App tip · not from the guideline" badge, in
  lighter text than the guideline's lines. A tip containing any number, or a quote, is flagged in Checks.
- New "Location:" box: a "Use GPS position" button fills in latitude/longitude (and degrees-minutes) with the
  accuracy and time, and saves it to the Log. Inside a claude.ai page the phone's location is blocked, so there it
  says so and you type the position; it works when the app file is opened as an ordinary web page.
- New "More:" lines fold background, AU/UK detail and source disagreements into one "More detail" section.
- Every app: one "Sources for this screen" fold replaces the "Guideline" link under every line. Record buttons
  say "Not tapped yet"; the Log now shows each answer with the step it was given on.
- New "Rescue" look (marine safety gear: high-vis orange and navy, big buttons, hazard-stripe warnings). App 18
  opens in it; every app can switch to it.
- App 18 reworked: most screens now show 2 to 4 lines. A second, fresh reviewer found 0 missed instructions and
  12 findings (a tip that gave crew-task advice, START hidden below the fold, the Mayday script needing a scroll,
  4 safety lines folded away that should stay visible); all fixed.
- Scores now: to fix 0, to check 0, all apps; app 18 94 of 94 quotes exact. Tests: 47 of 47 pass.

Gaps in the guideline (each said on screen):
- NZ sources differ on the wait before another dose: 2 to 5 min (Healthify), 3-5 min (kit), 2-3 min (The Level).
- How many doses: a second dose only (Healthify, kit) or no maximum (The Level). The kit has 5 ampoules; nobody
  says what to do when they run out.
- How long naloxone lasts: 20 minutes, or 20 to 90 minutes (both Healthify); 30 to 90 (Australian).
- No NZ source says how much to draw up, and the two disagree on which way the ampoule dot faces.
- No NZ rescue-breathing technique or CPR steps; follow the 111 call-taker.
