Latest run

Scheduled build run, 21 Nov 2026 (third run of the day).

Your feedback: nothing new on the design review page or on any app.

Built:
- App 20: School or early learning lockdown (NZ). Main source: Ministry of Education "Planning and preparing for
  emergencies" (October 2026 edition), with the Ministry's school, early learning and parent pages and NZ Police
  "Escape. Hide. Tell.". The January 2025 edition is used only to show where wording changed, labelled on screen.
  - First screen is one question: an attacker is near me; I am staff and heard the alert; I am calling a lockdown
    or shelter in place; Police told us; or we got a threat by email, post or phone.
  - Leader route: lockdown or shelter in place, alert the principal and staff, call 111 only if Police don't
    already know, check everyone is inside and type the names of anyone unaccounted for, lock up, tell staff,
    parents, off-site groups and the Ministry, stay inside until lifted, reunite children, debrief, Traumatic
    Incident team.
  - Classroom route: get inside, lock the doors, roll check, stay put, then follow the principal.
  - Attacker route: Police's Escape, Hide, Tell, with a way back from Hide to Escape.
  - 30 Record buttons and 11 typing boxes, so the Log keeps every time, name and decision for Police and the debrief.
  - Opens in the Flight deck look (dark, so the screen gives off less light in a room where people are hiding).
- Reviewer found 5 missed instructions (incl. "try to stop others entering the area", alert the principal and
  staff, remind parents of the pick-up process) and 15 findings (111 shown even when Police started it, a teacher
  sent to the principal's tasks, start screen too long). All fixed; 2 partly (see review.md).
- Scores: to fix 0, to check 0, all apps. 166 of 166 quotes exact (web copy). Tests: 49 of 49 pass.
- Seen working: phone-size screenshots of the start, alert and roll-check screens in Flight deck and Wayfinding.

Building blocks added: none. Wished for: hiding an answer based on an earlier choice (so a shelter-in-place
route never sees a "lockdown" answer).

Gaps in the guideline (each said on screen):
- No national alert or all-clear signal: each school's plan sets its own.
- Nothing on whether to open the door to someone knocking, or children who are outside or in the toilets.
- No times at all: how long to stay, how often to update parents.
- No incident-report form. The 2025 and 2026 editions word the roll check differently.

  and a second person overboard app (https://claude.ai/artifact/SGrGHN5zWwJAsLv7jbwwQq).
