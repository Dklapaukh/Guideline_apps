# School lockdown

About: For staff at a New Zealand school or early learning service in a lockdown: alert, call 111, roll check, lock, stay inside, all-clear, reunite. Keeps the times.

## Steps

### start: Lockdown: what is happening?
Ask: What is happening?
- An attacker is near me now -> attack
- I am staff: I heard the lockdown alert -> room
- I am calling a lockdown or shelter in place (principal, manager or delegate) -> called
- Police or another service told us to lock down or shelter in place -> told
- We got a threat by email, post or phone -> threat

### threat: Threat received: call Police
Do: Call NZ Police: 111 for emergencies, 105 if it is not an emergency.
Quote: "If your school receives a threat by email, post or phone, call NZ Police." (Ministry schools page)
Quote: "Call 111 for emergency incidents." (Ministry schools page)
Quote: "Call 105 for non-emergency incidents." (Ministry early learning page)
Record: Police called about the threat
More: Police will assess it, decide if it is credible, and tell you what to do. This may include a lockdown, evacuation or another safety measure.
Quote: "Police will assess the information you give them, determine whether it is credible, and tell you what actions you need to take." (Ministry schools page)
Quote: "This may include a lockdown, evacuation or another safety measure." (Ministry schools page)
More: Not an emergency? The early learning page says you can also complete an online report.
Quote: "You can also complete an online report for non-emergency events." (Ministry early learning page)
Ask: What did Police say?
- Lock down -> lockdown
- Shelter in place -> shelter
- Not told yet, or limited information -> called
- Something else (evacuate or another measure) -> other

### other: Not a lockdown
Say: Respond as your emergency management plan sets out, and follow Police.
Quote: "Respond to event as outlined in the emergency management plan" (Ministry guide 2026)
Quote: "tell you what actions you need to take." (Ministry schools page)
Tip: This app covers lockdown and shelter in place only.
End: Follow your emergency management plan.

### told: Told to lock down or shelter in place
Say: Note who told you, then start it.
Quote: "Instruction to lockdown/shelter in place received from: [Police/emergency services/we initiated it ourselves]" (Ministry guide 2026)
Record: Instruction received
Write: Instruction received from
More: An instruction can come from Police, the principal or delegate, the early childhood service manager or person responsible, other emergency services, or the Ministry of Education (at the direction of Police or another emergency service).
Quote: "Instructions to shelter in place or lockdown may be initiated by:" (Ministry guide 2026)
Quote: "the Ministry of Education (at the direction of the Police or other emergency service)." (Ministry guide 2026)
More: If Police started it, they advise when it is lifted, through the principal or manager or through the Ministry.
Quote: "If the Police initiated the shelter in place or lockdown, they would provide advice on lifting the shelter in place or lockdown directive through the principal or early childhood service manager directly or through the Ministry of Education." (Ministry guide 2026)
More: 2025 edition: Police will usually say "lockdown" rather than "shelter in place".
Quote: "Police will usually direct you to lockdown rather than use the term ‘shelter in place’." (2025 edition, replaced)
Ask: Which were you told?
- Lockdown -> lockdown
- Shelter in place -> shelter

### called: Lockdown or shelter in place?
Say: Limited information? Lock down until Police or emergency services tell you more.
Quote: "If you have limited information at the time you are advised of an incident/event, implementing a lockdown is advised until you get further information from the Police or other emergency services." (Ministry guide 2026)
More: Lockdown: the threat is directly related to your school or centre, for example an attacker on your grounds.
Quote: "Lockdown – issued when the threat is directly related to your school/centre." (Ministry guide 2026)
Quote: "For example, an attacker on your property/grounds." (Ministry guide 2026)
More: Shelter in place: the threat is external and not directed at you, for example wasps, toxic smoke, a chemical spill or an offender nearby.
Quote: "Shelter in place – issued when the threat is external and not directed at your school/centre." (Ministry guide 2026)
Quote: "For example, if there is a swarm of wasps, toxic smoke, chemical spill, or a threat from an offender nearby" (Ministry guide 2026)
More: If you made the decision, you lift it yourself.
Quote: "If you made the decision to shelter in place or lockdown, you would lift the directive yourself." (Ministry guide 2026)
Ask: Which one?
- Lockdown: threat aimed at us (e.g. attacker on grounds) -> lockdown
- Shelter in place: threat outside, not aimed at us (e.g. wasps, smoke) -> shelter
- Not sure: lockdown until Police say more -> lockdown

## Notes
Source copy: web-fetch only (education.govt.nz and police.govt.nz downloads blocked; see meta.json), so a quote not found may be the copy's fault. Lines in [square brackets] in source.txt are the researcher's notes and are never quoted, except in A2 and A9, where the brackets are the guide's own fill-in placeholders (quoted as printed).

Which source leads: the Ministry guide, October 2026 (A) is the main source; its example checklist (A9, page 45) sets the order of the lead route (instruction received, initiate, everyone inside and names unaccounted for, secure, notify Police, staff channel, off-site groups, parents, Ministry local office, keep in contact, toilets/first aid/food, step down to shelter in place, come out, reunite or resume). Escape. Hide. Tell. comes from A5 (the guide) with the extra Police lines from E (general public advice for an armed attack in a crowded place, not written for schools). The January 2025 edition (F) is superseded: it is used only in More lines that start "2025 edition:", never as the instruction.

Police step (A9 "Notify Police or other emergency services (if relevant)"): after the alert, the lead route asks whether Police or emergency services already know. "No" goes to call111 (quoting A1 "Call Emergency Services if needed - 111" and A5 "when it is safe"); "Yes, or not needed for this event" (for example Police started it, or wasps) goes on to the roll check, and the answer is in the Log. A9 lists notifying Police after the roll check and securing; A1's basic process lists calling 111 before responding, so the app asks straight after the alert. The attack route calls 111 on tell111 and then goes to lockdown-attack, which skips the Police question, so "111 called" is logged once.

Routes: start asks only "What is happening?" (no definition first; the definitions are in More on called and in its answers): attacker near now (Escape, Hide (with a way back to Escape when a way out opens), Tell 111, then alert-staff: A1 "Notify service provider/principal and all staff", then who are you: principal, manager or delegate -> lockdown-attack; lockdown already called -> hold; staff -> room-hold); staff who heard the alert (room route: inside, lock the doors, roll, stay put, bathroom or first aid, knock, shelter in place, all-clear, then follow the principal's instructions (room-end) or help with collection (release)); calling it (lockdown, shelter in place, or not sure: lockdown); told by Police or another service (note who, then lockdown or shelter in place, A4 lists Police and other emergency services for both); threat by email, post or phone (B: call NZ Police; answers lockdown, shelter in place, not told yet or limited information (to called, A4 "limited information ... lockdown is advised"), or something else). The lead chain (alert, Police, roll, secure, staff, tell, hold) is shared by lockdown and shelter in place, so its wording covers both ("Secure your locations as required. This may include locking doors and entrances", "Lockdown: advised we can move to shelter in place", "Lockdown or shelter in place lifted"); the runtime cannot hide an answer by an earlier choice. School and early learning differ only in drills (A6) and in who may collect a child (A8), so there is no school/early learning question; the release screen shows both. Drills are not in the app (it is for a real event). Gap: the guide does not list a classroom teacher's own steps after the all-clear (room-end says so).

Guideline gaps (said on screen, trap 13): no national alert signal ("sounding airhorn 3 times" is only A9's fill-in example; alert); no national all-clear signal or wording (A3 "How will you signal the end"; lifted, room-lifted); no rule on opening a locked door to a knock (knock: says so, only "remain at the required location until the further notice"); nothing on children outside or in the toilets when the alert goes (roll, room); no times or durations at all, so there are no timers or reminders (hold, room-hold); no interval for updating parents or off-site groups ("regularly"; tell); no incident-report form or deadline (summary). The Ministry and Police sources do not say to close blinds or curtains or turn lights off, so the app does not either. The staff "Not told yet" answer goes to a screen with A3's "they will need to know" and A4's "limited information: lockdown is advised"; A4's sentence is written for whoever decides, so the screen says "the guide advises" and asks the staff member to check the staff channel.

2025 vs 2026 differences (shown as "2025 edition:" More lines): roll check "take attendance, if possible, and prepare a list of missing and extra children" (F) vs "Check that everyone is inside. Note any names of anyone who is unaccounted for." (A9; the Write box follows A9: names unaccounted for); bathroom with "a designated staff member" and permission, camp toilets (F) vs "accompanied by an adult" (A4); "remain in the room until further instruction ... from NZ Police or ... a formal announcement" (F) vs "until the further notice" (A4); move to the closest classrooms and lock doors immediately (F, on room); Police usually say lockdown (F, on told); shelter in place details: restricted entries, no one in or out until an all-clear, ventilation and doors and windows (F, on shelter and room-shelter). Drills: F says children should not be in drills for violent situations; A6 says school lockdown drills with students, early learning without children (not in the app).

Message templates (A2): the lockdown and "emergency has ended" texts are shown word for word in More (tell, reunite, resume) with "fill in the brackets". The template says "All [children/students] and staff are safe."; the app does not judge when that is true. The heading of the "emergency has ended" template was not captured.

Record buttons (times for the debrief and handover): Police called about the threat, Instruction received, Lockdown started (lockdown, lockdown-attack), Shelter in place started, Alert given, 111 called (call111, tell111), Roll checked (roll, room-roll), Locations secured, Staff updated, Parents messaged, Off-site groups told, Ministry local office told, Child left room (bathroom or first aid) (needs, room-needs), Moved to shelter in place, All-clear (lifted), Parents told where and when to collect, Parents told it has ended, Traumatic Incident team contacted, Alert heard, Room locked, Knock at the door, Shelter in place, All-clear heard, Escaped, Hiding, Principal and staff alerted. Step titles never repeat a Record label, so the Log does not show the same words twice. Write boxes: Instruction received from (told), Names of anyone unaccounted for (roll, room-roll), Who left and who went with them (needs, room-needs), Advised by (stepdown, A9 "Advised by Police/emergency services/other"), Lifted on whose advice (lifted, room-lifted, A4 "depends on who initiated"), Who collected each child (release, A8), Who is with me (escape, for the roll), Anything else to note (summary). No source says to note times; the Record buttons are the app's way of keeping them.

App tips (no numbers, no advice): lockdown, shelter and room (buttons keep the times), other (app covers lockdown and shelter in place only), room-roll (names typed are kept in the Log), summary and room-end (show the Log at the debrief). Escape. Hide. Tell. lines are labelled as Police's advice on hide, room-lock and secure (A5 says it is Police guidance; E is the Police page).

Look: Flight deck, the dark look, so the screen gives off less light in a darkened room where people are hiding quietly. This is the app's design choice, not guideline advice.

Left out on purpose: lockdown kit and supply planning (A4 equipment list, except medicines in More on needs), drills (A6), Police RIOD plans (B), parents' page advice (C; written for parents), phone protocol planning (A2), verbal warning examples ("intruder in C block", A5 things to consider). A2's social media line is in More on room-hold; B2's online report for non-emergency events is in More on threat.
