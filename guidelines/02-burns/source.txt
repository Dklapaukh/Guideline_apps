Quick help
Check that the person is happy for you to touch them before you give first aid
Call 111 for an ambulance if the area of the burn is larger than half the person’s arm;
Cool the burn by pouring cool liquid over it for at least 20 minutes.
Cover the burn by using a non-adhesive dressing or loose cling film.

DO NOT
Put small children or babies into a cold bath or shower for a full 20 minutes

First: Cool the burn
Remember to cool the burn not the child, so use cool (NOT cold) water.
Pour cool water over the burn for at least 20 minutes.

Then: Cover the burn
Don’t use cling film on the face.

Chemical burns
Call 111 for an ambulance urgently.
Soak the area with lots of water
Very carefully try to crack the bitumen with scissors taking care not to damage the skin underneath

Burns in mouth or throat due to smoke or gas inhalation
Move the person to a safe area, preferably outside into fresh air.

Closing line
If you have a person in urgent need of medical attention, call 111 now.
