## Findings

1. **Two gaps that Notes says are "said in the app" are hidden (must fix, trap 13).** Where: `start` line 27, `mild` line 40, and `chest-thrusts` line 90. These are `Note:` lines, and `Note:` is author-only (docs/DESIGN.md: "for authors, never shown"). So the helper is never told: (a) that the guideline does not say what an effective cough looks like, (b) that it gives no time limit before "not relieved" means call an ambulance, or (c) that this app does not explain where the CPR compression point is. `## Notes` says "Each is said in the app", which is not true for (a) and (b). Fix: make each one a `Say:` line on its screen: `Say: The guideline does not describe what an effective cough looks like.` on `start` (don't add advice on what to do if unsure, because the source gives none), `Say: The guideline does not say how long to wait before calling an ambulance.` on `mild`, and `Say: The guideline points to the CPR compression point but does not describe it here.` on `chest-thrusts`. Keep the `Note:` lines for authors, or drop them. Correct the Notes sentence so it is true.

2. **"Start CPR" is added to the guideline's instruction (should fix; missed instruction: changed).** Where: `unresponsive` line 111. Source: "If the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, follow ANZCOR Basic Life Support Flowchart (Guideline 8)." The app says "start CPR following the ANZCOR Basic Life Support Flowchart". The flowchart begins with more than CPR (checking danger, response and breathing). "Start CPR" is general knowledge, not the quoted line, and CLAUDE.md rule 3 forbids filling it in. The template's own Note admits that it adds this. Fix: `Warning: If they are unresponsive and not breathing normally, follow the ANZCOR Basic Life Support Flowchart (Guideline 8). This app does not cover it.` If the owner wants the word CPR on screen, it needs a quote from Guideline 8, which is not in source.txt.

3. **The loops and the mild screen have no way out when help arrives (should fix, usability).** Where: `mild-call`, `back-blows`, `chest-thrusts`. If the obstruction never clears and the person stays responsive (or keeps coughing well), the only answers are cleared, worse, or loop again. The helper can never end the session or hand over, so the log never records what happened at handover. Fix: add one answer to each of the three screens, `- The ambulance has arrived -> handover`, and a short `handover` step with `Say: Tell the ambulance staff what you have done. Follow their advice.` and `End:`. That is app navigation, not a clinical instruction, so it needs no quote. It matches the wording already used on `cleared`. The Copy log on the finish screen then gives the staff the count of rounds.

4. **The "Partly blocked" and "Fully blocked" labels are not in the copied source (should fix, check).** Where: `start` lines 17 and 21. source.txt lists the six signs as plain lines, with no "partial" or "complete" headings. The template groups them into two labelled sets. That is probably right (ANZCOR 3.0 is set out that way), but the copy cannot prove it, and the build's quote check cannot catch a wrong grouping. Fix: recapture the two 3.0 sub-headings into source.txt (or save the PDF in sources/) and quote them. Until then, add a line to Notes: "The partial/complete grouping is from the guideline layout and is not in our copy."

5. **The first screen is too long for a panicked helper, and its signs don't connect to its question (should fix, usability).** Where: `start`. There are five Say lines (about 70 words, plus six quote boxes) before "Can they cough well?". The blocked-airway signs are about breathing, and the question is about cough, so the helper has to make the link alone. Fix: keep only "Choking is a life-threatening emergency" and "The simplest check is whether they can cough well" above the Ask. Move the clutching sign and the partly and fully blocked signs to `About:`, or to a line under the Ask headed "Signs of choking". Don't add a mapping (for example, "fully blocked = cough not working"), because the source does not give one.

6. **The ambulance line on the severe screen adds "or call yourself" (minor).** Where: `severe` line 58. Source: "send for an ambulance". "(or call yourself)" is sensible for someone alone, but it is not in the source, and calling may delay back blows. Fix: `Do: Send for an ambulance now.`. If the owner wants the lone-helper case covered, add `Say: The guideline does not say what to do if you are alone.` Also consider putting the ambulance line at the top of `back-blows` and dropping the separate `severe` screen. That saves one tap before the first blow. The abdominal-thrust and "rapid sequence" lines can move to `back-blows` too, as small Say lines.

7. **Soft recommendations are made into commands (minor, trap 8).** Where: `severe` line 60 ("Don't use abdominal thrusts", from "not recommended") and `unresponsive` line 105 ("Don't sweep blindly", from "ANZCOR suggests against"). Both are fair plain-English versions, and neither changes what a helper should do. But "suggests against" is a weak recommendation in ANZCOR's wording. Fix (optional): "Abdominal thrusts are not recommended." and "Avoid blind finger sweeps inside their mouth."

8. **Unresponsive but breathing normally is not covered, and that gap is not said (minor, trap 13).** Where: `unresponsive`. The source covers only "unresponsive and not breathing normally". A helper whose person is unresponsive but breathing has no instruction and isn't told so. Fix: add `Say: This part of the guideline does not say what to do if they are unresponsive but breathing normally.` Don't add what to do instead, because the source gives nothing. Add it to the Gaps line in Notes. Also consider showing the BLS line first on this screen. It is the most urgent action, and the source's order here (finger sweeps, extraction, BLS) is a list, not a sequence.

9. **The "Record" buttons add a tap with no use while giving blows (minor, usability).** Where: `back-blows`, `chest-thrusts`. The helper's hands are on the person. The answer buttons already log each round (the finish log shows every round). Fix (optional): remove the two `Record:` lines, or keep them only if the owner wants a visible round counter.

10. **Small wording (minor).** `mild` splits "Reassure them" and "Encourage them to keep coughing" into two Do lines, so the first shows as unquoted in Checks. Merge them into one line: "Reassure them and encourage them to keep coughing to bring the object up." On `mild`, the Warning "If it doesn't clear, call an ambulance" and the answer "It hasn't cleared ... -> mild-call" say the same thing twice. Keep the answer and drop the Warning, or keep both. Either is fine.

Missed instructions: 1 ("follow ANZCOR Basic Life Support Flowchart" changed to "start CPR following ...", finding 2). Nothing else in source.txt is missing or changed in meaning. The infant lines and the 4.0 evidence lines are left out on purpose, and Notes explains why.
