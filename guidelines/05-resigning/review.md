# Review: 05-resigning (independent, 18 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Faithful and careful. Every quote is exact, nothing is invented, no suspect example date is used, and every route works. But the app leaves out the most important protection for an employee whose employer wants them gone early: they must still be paid for the full notice period. Two other routes either hide or leave out the rule a person needs. Fix finding 1 before publishing, and ideally findings 2 to 5 too.**

## Findings

1. **Missing: if your employer wants you to leave early, they must still pay you for the full notice period (must fix; missed instruction).** Where: `during`, `in-lieu`, `shorter`. Source B: "If the employer wants the employee to work a shorter notice period, the employee must agree." "If the employee does not agree, the employer must still pay them for the full notice period, as long as: the employee works it, or another arrangement is agreed, for example, garden leave or payment instead of notice." Source C: "Even if the employee agrees not to work for some or all the notice period, their employer must still pay them for the full notice period." None of this is in the app. The only pay rule on the "leaving earlier" route is the opposite case: "If you ask to leave early and your employer agrees, you are only paid for the part of the notice you actually worked." An employee being pushed out early may read that line and accept less pay. The source makes the difference turn on who asks. Fix: add an answer to `during`, "My employer wants me to leave earlier", leading to a short step. On it: you must agree (quote B 298); if you don't agree, they must still pay you for the full notice period, as long as you work it or another arrangement is agreed (quote B 299 to 301); and even if you agree not to work some or all of it, they must still pay you for the full notice period (quote C 403). Then go to `in-lieu` or `during`. On `in-lieu`, add the C 403 line under "only if you agree". On `shorter`, begin the pay line with "If **you** ask to leave early...", in bold or as a Warning, so the two cases can't be mixed up.

2. **The "shorter notice not agreed" rule is wrong for people with no notice period in their agreement (should fix; missed instruction).** Where: `shorter`. The app says only "If they don't agree, you must work the notice period in your employment agreement." The source goes on: "If there’s no notice period in the agreement, they must work the notice period that was: previously agreed by the employer and employee, or given when notice was first provided." A person on the reasonable-notice route has no period in their agreement, so the screen gives them no rule. Fix: add this as a Say, quoting all three lines (B 247 to 249).

3. **"I don't feel safe working my notice" is hidden (should fix).** Where: `unsafe` can only be reached through `during` → "I want to leave earlier" → `shorter` → "Is there more to it?". A frightened person is unlikely to look there, and the source places this line under "How much notice to give", not under shorter notice. Fix: add "I don't feel safe working my notice" as an answer on `during`. Merging the two "leave early" answers would keep the menu at five. Also consider adding it on `issues`. Keep the gap line ("The pages do not say what happens next").

4. **Parental leave: the letter screen tells you to work out a last day the parental rule has already set (should fix).** Where: `parental` → `leave-counted` → `write`. `parental` says "Your last day of employment is the day your parental leave started". Then `leave-counted` says "work out your last day of employment" and `write` says "Give your last day of employment (based on your notice period)". For this person the two clash, and the date may already be in the past. The source does not say what date to put in the letter in this case. Fix: on `parental`, add "The pages do not say what last day to write in your resignation in this case. Ask your employer or Employment New Zealand (0800 20 90 20)." Add it to Notes too. Also consider sending `parental` and `defence` straight to `write`: "time on leave is not counted in the notice period" makes little sense for someone already on long leave. That choice is the owner's.

5. **The annual holiday pay screens ask a choice the person can't make, and other-pay is too long (should fix).** Where: `holiday-pay`, `holiday-case-1`, `holiday-case-2`, `other-pay`. The honest handling of the lost labels is right (no guessing). But "Which case do you want to read?" asks a stressed person to choose between two unlabelled cases, and each case repeats the same warning. `other-pay` mixes four topics in about 105 words (alternative holidays, sick and bereavement leave, ACC, other payments). `public-holidays` is the longest screen (about 110 words). Fix: (a) Recapture the Final pay page (save it as a PDF in sources/) to get the two tab labels. That is the real fix, and the owner should be asked. (b) Until then, put both cases on one screen, headed "The page shows two ways; our copy lost which is for whom". Show the warning once, then the two cases, then Next to `public-holidays`. That removes one decision and two screens. (c) Split `other-pay` into an Ask ("Do any of these apply? Owed alternative holidays / Unused sick or bereavement leave / Been on ACC / None"), or into two screens.

6. **Several smaller employee instructions are left out (minor; missed instructions).**
   - B 294: "If an employee wants to work from home during the notice period, they must talk to their employer or ask for flexible working arrangements." Add it to `during` or `leave-in-notice`.
   - B 288 to 291 (the handover list: finish outstanding tasks, hand over ongoing work, organise and record files, tools and processes, prepare for your last day). The app condenses this to "agree a plan for handing over your work". Add the list as Do lines on `last-day`, or on a short screen after "No, I'll work my notice".
   - A 102 to 105 and B 284: both sides "must continue to meet their employment rights and responsibilities", and "the employer must continue to keep the employee’s personal details private". Only the employee's confidentiality duty is shown (on `last-day`). Add the employer's privacy duty on the same screen, since it reassures the employee.
   - A 123 and A 126 to 127: a secondary-employment clause "must" have "a genuine reason". If there is no such clause, "Both parties should consider all the circumstances and act in good faith." Add both to `new-job`.
   - B 322: "An employer can only make an employee take annual holidays or alternative holidays in certain situations." Add it to `leave-in-notice` with "The pages do not say which situations."
   - C 446 to 451 (ACC): the rate ("the higher of the employee's: average weekly earnings for the 12 months before their end date, or ordinary weekly pay") and "Being on ACC is considered continuous employment" are left out of `other-pay`. Add them to the ACC line.

7. **"Must act in good faith" is softened (minor).** Where: `notice-reasonable`. Source: "Employers and employees must act in good faith when deciding what’s reasonable. This means discussing together how long reasonable notice should be and why." Only the second sentence is quoted, as a Do. Fix: begin the Do with "You and your employer must act in good faith:", quoting B 228.

8. **The two pages give slightly different rules for "not enough notice" (minor, trap 5).** Where: `short-notice`. Source B: the employer only has to pay for days "actually worked" **and** approved paid leave or holidays. Source C: "they only have to pay the employee for the days they actually worked." The app uses B, the fuller rule, which is a reasonable choice, but Notes does not mention C. Fix: add a line to Notes. Optionally, quote C 407 under the Warning.

9. **Examples of last-day tasks are turned into commands for everyone (minor).** Where: `last-day`. Source: "For example, organising to return company property or setting up ‘out of office’ messages." The app shows "Do: Return company property" and "Do: Set up 'out of office' messages", which won't fit everyone (for example, a casual worker with no work email). Fix: make it one line, "Do: Finish your last-day tasks, for example returning company property or setting up 'out of office' messages."

10. **The `issues` answers don't fit someone who has a problem and wants to talk first (minor).** Where: `issues`. The answers are "No, or I've already tried that", "I feel forced" and "Finish for now". A person who answers "yes, there's a problem, and I'll talk to my employer first" has to pick "Finish for now". Fix: add "Yes: I'll talk to my employer first -> end", or relabel "Finish for now" as "I'll talk to my employer first".

11. **The 0800 number is used outside its context, and is missing where the app says "call" (minor).** Where: `forced` gives "Get advice: Employment New Zealand's number is 0800 20 90 20" for a personal grievance. On the page, the number sits under final pay problems ("If that does not resolve the problem, or they want advice"). Notes says so, which is fine. But `defence` says "call Employment New Zealand" and gives no number. Fix: add the number to `defence` with the same quote, or drop "call" there.

12. **Notes overstates the bad dates (minor).** Notes says the Rama and Megan examples are not shown because "some dates look wrong". Rama's and Megan's dates are self-consistent (Monday 10 November, then Friday 5 December, as in 2025). Only the 25 May / 21 June example and the Jason example are suspect. Fix: correct the Note. The Rama example could then be used on `leave-in-notice` to explain "last day at work vs last day of employment" (B 192 to 203). The roster case (B 196) is not shown at all, and could go there in one line.

13. **Casual workers are never told plainly that they must resign (minor).** Where: `casual`. Source: "They must resign when they: accept an offer of work, and want to leave before completing that work." Also: "If they want to permanently end the employment relationship with that employer, they can resign at any time." The answers imply both, but neither is said or quoted. Fix: add both as Say lines with their quotes before the Ask. Also, `not-resigning` shows the fixed-term and casual sentences to both groups. Splitting it into two small screens, or adding a lead-in for each, would read better.

14. **Screen count (minor).** 40 screens is more than needed, but most are short, and no single route goes above about 17. These merges lose no instruction: put `fixed-term-early`'s single Do on `fixed-term` (above the Ask) and send "Leave before the agreed end date" to `issues`; merge the two holiday cases (finding 5b); fold `check-pay`'s question onto the end of `other-pay` (or the new last holiday screen). That gives about 36 screens. Findings 1 and 3 add one screen back. The overall order (agreement → leave → notice → leave counted → write → employer reply → notice period → last day → final pay) matches the source and should stay.

Missed instructions: 8 (employer-initiated shortening still paid in full, finding 1; the no-notice-period fallback, finding 2; and six minor ones in finding 6: working from home, the handover list, continuing rights and privacy, genuine reason and good faith for secondary-employment clauses, being made to take holidays, and the ACC rate)
