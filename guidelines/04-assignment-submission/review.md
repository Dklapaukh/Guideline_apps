# Review: 04-assignment-submission (independent, 18 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: Faithful, every quote exact, and all routes work. But three routes skip the "no name, Word file" checks, a hard-copy student is told "Your assignment is submitted", and the technical-problem screen asks for a receipt the student cannot have yet. Fix those before publishing.**

Checked against source.txt (Sections A and B). Build: 15 steps, all reachable, 0 fix, 0 check, 10 info, 53/53 quotes exact. Fake browser: the explorer ran 119 routes with 24 endings, reached all 15 steps and found no problems. By hand I tried all done, hard copy, not sure, confirm "No" then technical then "Try uploading again", wrong file, late then extension, extension from the start, and technical then Finish. There were no page errors, and every route ends. No penalties, times or rules are invented: 5 marks, calendar days, weekends, 12 noon and the email address are all in the source.

## Findings

1. **Three "go back and upload" routes skip the Before-you-submit checks (must fix).** Where: `late` → "Go to the upload steps" → `open-assignment`; `wrong-file` → "Upload the right file" → `upload`; `technical` → "Try uploading again" → `open-assignment`. A student who starts from "Something went wrong" never sees "All assignments submitted must be anonymised, i.e. with NO STUDENT NAMES." or "The assignment must be submitted in Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) format." This matters most on the late route, because a late student is often rushing, and the source says "penalties apply for incomplete/incorrect submissions". Fix: point "Go to the upload steps" (on `late`) and "Upload the right file" (on `wrong-file`) to `checks`. On `technical`, either do the same or add one line: "Before you upload again: no name on it, and saved in Word (.doc or .docx)", quoting both lines.

2. **The hard-copy route ends with "Your assignment is submitted" (must fix).** Where: `hard-copy` → `checks` → … → `done`. The student has uploaded only the digital copy. "Your assignment is submitted" may lead them not to hand in the hard copy their course requires. Conditions can only test inputs, not the route taken, so fix it with a plain line on `done`: "If your course also wants a hard copy, you still need to hand that in. The guidance does not say how. Check your course outline or Canvas." Quote "In some courses, assignments are submitted in hard copy and digital format." Also soften the first line to "Your digital copy is submitted."

3. **The technical screen asks for a receipt the student cannot have yet (should fix).** Where: `technical`. "You will need a copy of your receipt of submission." is shown to someone whose upload is failing, so there is no receipt yet. An anxious student may read this as "I can't challenge anything". The source does not say what to do in this case. Fix: change the wording to "If the upload is late because of a technical problem, you can only challenge the late penalty if you can prove a technical malfunction. You will need the receipt from your submission once it goes through, so download it then." Keep both quotes. Add a line to Notes: "The guidance does not say how to prove a malfunction when no submission went through." Also say on screen what the source leaves open: if the due time has already passed, the source still says to contact the lecturer only "prior to the submission due date and time". Add "The guidance does not say what to do if the due time has already passed."

4. **The preview check has no way out if the file is wrong (should fix).** Where: `accept`. The screen says "Make sure it is the right file", then "You must click it", with only a Next button. A student who sees the wrong file has no route. The source says nothing about what to do in this case. Fix: turn the screen into an Ask: "Is it the right file?" Yes → a short screen (or a reordered `accept`) with the blue "Accept submission – save" button line. No → a step that says "The guidance does not say what to do if the preview shows the wrong file. Contact your lecturer before the due date and time." Quote the lecturer line, then give the option to go to `wrong-file`. Note that "make sure it is the right file" is the app's own wording: the source only says "you can see a preview". Say so in Notes.

5. **The extension timing is unclear (should fix, trap 6).** Where: `extension`. Source: "To apply for an extension, you must: (i) email lawextensions@auckland.ac.nz (ii) supply evidence (e.g. doctor’s certificate) before the due date." The app attaches "before the due date" to the evidence only, so the email line has no timing. It is unclear whether the source means before the due date applies to the whole application. It also says "before the due date", not "before 12 noon on the due date". Fix: put one line first, "Apply before the due date.", quoting "To apply for an extension, you must:" with the evidence line. Then add to the Say and to Notes: "The guidance says 'before the due date'. It does not say whether that means before the due day or before 12 noon on it. To be safe, ask early." (This last part is advice. If that counts as invention, leave out "To be safe, ask early" and keep only the gap.)

6. **Take-home tests are only mentioned on the late screen (should fix; missed instruction).** Where: `format`, `late`. Source: "You will be advised about applicable penalties and the format of submission of take-home tests in the course outlines and/or via Canvas." This covers format as well as penalties. A take-home-test student who taps "Submit my assignment" is never told to check the format in the course outline. On `late`, the take-home line comes after the 5-mark rule and the example, so that student reads the wrong penalty first. Fix: add an answer to `format`, "It's a take-home test", leading to a short step with both take-home quotes, then Next: `checks`. On `late`, move the take-home line to the top, or begin the screen with "Not a take-home test? Then:".

7. **The late penalties are for online-only courses, but the screen does not say what applies otherwise (minor).** Where: `late`. The screen begins "For online-only courses", which is right, but a hard-copy-course student is left guessing. Notes already says the source gives no penalties for hard-copy courses. Fix: add "The guidance gives no late penalties for courses that want a hard copy. Check your course outline."

8. **"Recommended" becomes a command, and the "differ" note overstates the conflict (minor).** Where: `confirm`. Source A says "You are recommended to download and save a copy of the digital receipt". Source B says "(if you wish to do so; the Faculty does not require this)". These do not really contradict each other: recommended is not the same as required. But "Do: Download your digital receipt" followed by "The two parts of the guidance differ" may worry a student. Fix: write "Recommended: download your digital receipt and save a copy. It is not required, but you need it if you ever challenge a late penalty." Drop the "differ" sentence, or soften it, and keep the Note.

9. **The late screen is long for a panicking student (minor).** Where: `late`. It has six Say blocks plus a menu. Fix: put the option the student most needs first (upload now, or ask for an extension if the due date has not passed). Move the worked example below the take-home line, or onto its own "How is it worked out?" screen.

10. **"No hard copy submission is required" for online-only courses is not shown (minor).** Source: "No hard copy submission is required." (under online-only). This would reassure the "Online only, through Canvas" route. Fix: add it as a Say with its quote on `checks`, or on a short screen after the "Online only" answer. The same applies to "The assignment must be uploaded via the Canvas assignment page." (it is implied by the steps but never quoted).

11. **The Google Drive or Dropbox option conflicts with the Word rule (minor).** Where: `upload`. A student picking a file from Google Drive may pick a Google Doc, not a .docx. Fix: add "Whichever you choose, the file must be Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx)." with the Word quote.

The checks that passed: "You can only challenge…" keeps "only" (trap 10). "Lecturers and tutors do not and cannot grant extensions" is kept whole, and the app explains that this does not clash with "contact their lecturer" about technical problems. "(or part of day thereof)", "calendar day basis (not a 24-hour basis)" and the weekend rule are all shown (trap 6). The worked example matches the source (2pm Thursday: 5 marks; 5am Friday: 10 marks), and the Example 2 table agrees with it. The "both in digital copy" oddity, the unclosed bracket and the undated source are stated in Notes, and Version is filled (trap 12). Gaps (does a new upload replace the old one, a last day for late work, hard-copy instructions, word limits, help desk) are stated on screen. The LLB-only audience is on the start screen and in the banner. "Reviewed by" is empty, and the prototype banner is present. Each screen asks one question, with plain answer labels.

**Missed instructions: 1** (the take-home-test format pointer, "You will be advised about … the format of submission of take-home tests in the course outlines and/or via Canvas", is missing from the submit route. See finding 6.)
