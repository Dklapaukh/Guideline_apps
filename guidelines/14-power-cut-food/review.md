**Verdict: faithful and careful with the sources. No invented times, temperatures or food rules, every disagreement is shown side by side, and every "not sure" answer goes to the safer screen. One route problem must be fixed before the owner sees it: while the power is still off, the app ends ("That's all for now", or after the fridge, pantry and hygiene screens) and there is no way back to "the power is back on" without starting again, which wipes the power-off time and the reminders. A few wording fixes are also needed (reminder wording, ARPHS "after today", ARPHS bread on a freezer screen).**

## What is right
- No invented numbers. Every time and temperature on screen is in source.txt: 2 hours, 4 hours / around four hours, 2–4 days, 1–2 days, 24 hours, 48 hours, 5°C, 10°C for over 8 hours, two hours at room temperature, and 0800 611 116. Ranges are shown as printed ("2–4 days", "1–2 days"), never as one value. The table's "-" cells show "Not stated in the guideline", not zero. Nothing about 4°C, thermometers, ice-cube tricks or a US "4 hours / 2 days" rule has crept in.
- Contradictions are shown, not settled: fridge time (4 h, around four hours, at least 2 h), freezer time (three columns), thawed food (MPI keep if it looks and smells fine; Northland don't use; ARPHS dispose after two hours at room temperature), ice cream and frozen yoghurt, thawed fruit, hard cheese against "cheese", and bread.
- "Do not", "only", "except" and "unless"-type words are kept whole: Northland's "Do not use frozen food that has thawed ... The only exceptions to this are ...", MPI's "and the packaging isn't damaged or open", FSANZ's "Don't taste it", MPI's "Do not use any tinned food that has been damaged", "(except cans)".
- Safe routing: "No, or I'm not sure" (ice crystals) goes to Thawed; "Not sure" (fridge time) goes to the warmed-up screen; damaged packaging falls back on "If in doubt, throw it out". After the power is back on the order is freezer, then fridge, then pantry, then hygiene, following MPI's "Once the power is back on, check the food in your freezer". "The power is back on" stops all reminders.
- Organisations are named correctly on every line; FSANZ is labelled as the joint Australia-New Zealand agency; the flood and fire lines are clearly marked "If there was flooding" / "If there was a fire".
- Gaps are said plainly on screen (no definition of full/half full, no way to tell how long or how warm, nothing on leftovers, formula, breast milk or insulin) with Healthline as the only contact, as the source gives it.

## Findings

1. **Dead end while the power is still off: the app finishes and there is no way to say "the power is back on".** Where: `during` (answer "That's all for now -> end"), and the power-still-off route `during` → `fridge` → `fridge-*` → `pantry` → `hygiene` (End). Seen in the fake browser: after "That's all for now" the Finished screen shows only "Copy log" and "Start again"; the 4-hour and 24-hour reminders keep firing above it ("NOW · 1:00:00 ago"); and "Start again" clears the Log, the "Power went off" time stamp and the reminders. A person who checks the app at the start of a cut and comes back when the power returns cannot reach `back-on` / `freezer` without losing the time they recorded. Source words the route needs to reach: "Once the power is back on, check the food in your freezer." (MPI fact sheet, NEMA, QLDC). Fix: replace "That's all for now -> end" with an answer that keeps the app open, for example a small `waiting` step ("Leave this app open. Come back when the power is back on.") with Ask: "The power has come back on -> back-on" / "Check the food in the fridge -> fridge". On `hygiene` (and `pantry`) add an answer "The power has come back on -> back-on" alongside Finish, so the power-still-off route can carry on. Check the explorer still finishes (it already stops at its 400-route limit for this app). Severity: **must fix**.

2. **Reminders don't say that time has passed, and one of them reads as more time left.** Where: `just-off`, the three `Remind:` lines. When the 2-hour reminder fires the bar reads "2 hours: Northland says an unopened fridge keeps food cool for at least 2 hours", which a stressed reader can take as "you have at least 2 more hours". The 4-hour one says "about 4 hours", but MPI, NEMA and QLDC say "4 hours" (only FSANZ says "around four hours"). Source words: "An unopened refrigerator will keep your food cool for at least 2 hours." (Northland); "Unopened fridges should hold refrigeration temperature for 4 hours." (MPI fact sheet); "A fridge should keep food cold for around four hours - after that it can begin to spoil." (FSANZ). Fix: start each reminder with what has happened: "2 hours since the power went off. Northland: an unopened fridge keeps food cool for at least 2 hours. Keep the doors shut." / "4 hours since the power went off. MPI, NEMA and QLDC: an unopened fridge holds its cold for 4 hours; FSANZ: around four hours, after that food can begin to spoil. Check the fridge food." / "24 hours since the power went off. FSANZ: a freezer shouldn't defrost for around 24 hours. Northland: a half full freezer is okay for up to 24 hours." Severity: **should fix**.

3. **ARPHS's "After today" is turned into "by the end of the first day".** Where: `fridge-warm` Say. This is a timing-word trap (LESSONS 6). ARPHS said "after today" in a news item on one day of a multi-day outage ("With some households in the region facing two or more days without electricity"); it does not say when the clock starts, so "by the end of the first day" is the author's reading. Source words: "After today, highly perishable foods in the fridge like meat and fish may not be safe to eat," Fix: "Auckland Regional Public Health Service (in a news item during a long power cut): 'after today', meat and fish from the fridge may not be safe. It gives no number of hours." Severity: **should fix**.

4. **ARPHS's bread line is about the fridge but is shown on the thawed-freezer bread screen.** Where: `thawed-baked` ("Auckland Regional Public Health Service says bread from the fridge can still be eaten."). On a screen about thawed frozen bread, cakes and custard pies, this line reads as "ARPHS says the thawed bread is fine", which ARPHS did not say (its freezer line is "Dispose of any food from the freezer which has thawed out and been at room temperature for longer than two hours."). Source words: "Throw out rotten or contaminated food quickly so it doesn't spoil other food in the fridge." / "You can still eat food like raw vegetables, cheese and bread." Fix: remove it from `thawed-baked` (it is already on `fridge-warm`) and instead show ARPHS's freezer line there, as on `thawed`. Severity: **should fix**.

5. **No freezer route while the power is still off.** Where: `during` (answers go only to fridge, back on, or end). The sources say eat the fridge first, then the freezer, during the cut, and ARPHS gives a freezer rule that applies before the power returns. A person on day two of a cut, eating from the freezer, gets no screen. Source words: "If the power goes out, eat the food from your fridge first, then your freezer." (Get Ready); "Dispose of any food from the freezer which has thawed out and been at room temperature for longer than two hours." (ARPHS). Fix: add "Check the food in the freezer -> thawed" (or a short `during-freezer` step) on `during`, worded so it does not talk about refreezing while the power is off; the `freezer` screen's "now the power is back on" wording should stay only on the back-on route. Severity: **should fix**.

6. **"Not sure how full" shows "Not stated in the guideline" for MPI and Northland, which helps no one.** Where: `## Tables`, Freezer hold time, row "Not sure how full".
